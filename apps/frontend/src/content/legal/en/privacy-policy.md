QuietJar operates the QuietJar website, the embedded widget, and the API behind
them. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and what rights you
have. Supporters can find this policy through the link in the widget. Our company
details and contact addresses are in the [imprint](/legal/imprint/).

## Who we are

QuietJar is a product of GoodFolk B.V. i.o., which operates the QuietJar website,
the widget, and the API. GoodFolk B.V. i.o. is the controller for the personal data
described in this policy.

Where we process donation data on behalf of a creator, that creator is the
controller and QuietJar is the processor. That processing is governed by the
[data processing addendum](/legal/data-processing-addendum/). For fee accounting,
platform security, and statutory bookkeeping, QuietJar is the controller itself.

## What data we collect

**Account data.** Your email address, which is all that registration requires.

**Authentication data.** One-time sign-in codes, which expire after five minutes,
and session records for the dashboard.

**Site and widget data.** The site address the widget runs on, the styling and copy
you configure, the country and currency you choose, and whether you receive
donations as an individual or a business.

**Payment data.** For each donation, the amount, currency, payment status,
timestamps, payment identifiers, and our fee, received from Stripe. Card and bank
details entered by a supporter go directly to Stripe and never reach us. Where you
open a payment account, Stripe collects your details and identity data as a
controller under its own privacy policy; we receive only the status of that setup.

**Suppression records.** The site, the date, and a pseudonymous identifier
(a random value containing no name, email, or card details), kept on the
supporter's device and on ours, so the widget steps back for a while after a
donation instead of asking again.

**Support data.** Anything you send us by email or in writing.

**Technical data.** Connection data such as IP address, user agent, and time of
request, processed by our infrastructure to deliver the site, widget, and API
securely.

## Why we process your data (purposes and legal basis)

- To operate your account, authenticate you, and serve the widget and API. Legal
  basis: performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- To account for our fee and keep statutory books and records. Legal basis:
  performance of contract and legal obligation, Articles 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR.
- To protect the service against fraud and abuse, enforce rate limits, and keep it
  secure and available. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure and
  reliable service, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- To answer your questions. Legal basis: performance of contract, or legitimate
  interest before a contract is formed.
- To comply with the law and assert or defend legal claims. Legal basis: legal
  obligation or legitimate interest.

We do not profile supporters, sell data, or use anything for advertising.

## How long we keep your data

**Payment and fee records.** Seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.

**Account data.** For the life of the account, then deleted within 30 days of
closure.

**Suppression records.** Kept only while the step-back is active, then deleted.

**Technical and security logs.** For brief, fixed periods, no longer than 30 days.

## Who we share your data with

Only with processors engaged to run the service, each bound by a data processing
agreement: our payment provider, our hosting and database provider, and our
transactional email provider. The current list, with roles and locations, is on
the [subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors/) page.

If you are a creator, your payment account is a contract between you and Stripe.
Identity checks for that account are processed by Stripe as a controller under its
own terms.

We share data with authorities only where the law requires it. We do not sell data
and share nothing for advertising.

## International data transfers

Processing takes place in the EEA, except that Stripe transfers data to the United
States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. If
any other processor were ever to process data outside the EEA, we would rely on an
adequacy decision or, failing that, on standard contractual clauses, and record
the safeguards on the subprocessors page before the change takes effect.

## How we protect your data

Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Payment details are entered directly
into Stripe's certified payment element inside the widget and never reach our
servers. Access to production systems is restricted to the personnel who need it,
under individual credentials, and logged. Sign-in uses one-time codes rather than
passwords.

## Your rights under the GDPR

You may request access to your data and its rectification, erasure, restriction, and
portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where
consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it at any time. To exercise a right,
write to privacy@quietjar.com. We respond within one month and may ask for proof of
identity.

If you donated to a website that uses QuietJar, we process your donation for that
website, not for ourselves. Direct your request to that website; we assist it in
answering you.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the
Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority, or the
supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## Cookies and tracking

QuietJar sets no analytics or advertising cookies. The dashboard sets strictly
necessary session cookies, described in the
[cookie policy](/legal/cookie-policy/). The widget itself sets no cookies.

## Children's privacy

The service is aimed at website owners. We do not knowingly process the personal
data of children, and registration is open only to persons aged 18 or older. On
the sites of the creators who use QuietJar, the creator's own privacy notice
applies to visitors.

## Changes to this policy

We publish every change on this page with a new last-updated date. If a change
affects processing described here in a material way, we notify account holders by
email beforehand.

## Contact us

Write to privacy@quietjar.com. Postal and company details are in the imprint.
