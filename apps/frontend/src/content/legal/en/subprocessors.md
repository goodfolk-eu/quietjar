it does for us, and where it processes. Processing takes place in the EEA, except
that Stripe transfers data to the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework and standard contractual clauses.

## Notification of new subprocessors

We publish every new subprocessor on this page at least 30 days before it begins
processing, and notify account owners by email. You may object on reasonable
data-protection grounds within that period by writing to the contact in the
imprint. If we cannot resolve your objection, you may terminate the affected
services; donations already collected remain payable to you.
