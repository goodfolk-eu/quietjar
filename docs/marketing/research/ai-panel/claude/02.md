# 02: Ko-fi vs Buy Me a Coffee, which is better?

**Short answer:** For most people, Ko-fi is better, mainly on fees. Buy Me a Coffee (BMAC) can still make sense in some cases.

**Fees**
- **Ko-fi:** It takes 0% of one-off tips on the free plan. It takes 5% on other sales, such as shop items and memberships, unless you pay for Ko-fi Gold. Sources disagree on Gold's price, with figures of $6/mo and $8/mo.
- **BMAC:** It takes 5% of everything, including tips, memberships and product sales.
- **Payment processing:** Both pass on processor fees of roughly 2.9% + $0.30.

**Choose Ko-fi if:**
- Most of your income will be tips or donations, since you keep more of each one.
- You want to start free, with no commitment.
- You sell a few digital products or commissions and want a simple shop.

**Choose Buy Me a Coffee if:**
- Your audience is mostly on mobile. One source says BMAC's native app may justify the 5% fee.
- You want supporters to be able to cover the processing fee at checkout. One source says BMAC offers this and Ko-fi doesn't.
- You prefer its simpler, more focused interface for memberships.

**Caveats:** Most of this comes from third-party comparison blogs. Two of them have a conflict of interest: UniLink sells link-in-bio tools, and SchoolMaker is a competing course platform. Fees and plans change often, so check both official pricing pages before you commit. I didn't verify the numbers there.
