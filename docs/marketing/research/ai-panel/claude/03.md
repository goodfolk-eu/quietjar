# 03: Is there a European alternative to Buy Me a Coffee or Patreon?

Yes, there are several. Here are the main European options. They come from listicle-style search results, so check each platform's current fees and payout countries before committing.

- **Liberapay** is a non-profit, open-source platform. It supports recurring donations, which is closer to Patreon than a one-off tip jar. It reportedly takes no cut of donations and funds itself through donations. It's a good fit if you want something simple and ethical, but it has fewer membership features.
- **Tipeee** is a French platform. It has tips, tiered support levels and community features, so it covers both the Buy Me a Coffee and Patreon use cases. One review says payouts are limited to certain European countries.
- **Steady** is a German platform aimed at publishers, newsletter writers, podcasters and other media creators. It helps turn readers, listeners and viewers into paying members. It's freemium.
- **Open Collective** is a freemium, open-source option that suits projects and communities. I couldn't confirm from the results where it's based, so check that if EU hosting matters to you.
- **Fangage** also came up as an alternative. The sources don't confirm it's European, so verify that first.

AlternativeTo has an [EU-only filter for Buy Me a Coffee alternatives](https://alternativeto.net/software/buy-me-a-coffee/?origin=eu), which is a quick way to see more options.
