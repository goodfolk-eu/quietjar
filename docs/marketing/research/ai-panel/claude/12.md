# 12: Should I use Stripe Payment Links or Ko-fi for small donations?

**Short version:** For small donations, Ko-fi is usually the better choice. Choose Stripe Payment Links if you want full control and no third-party branding.

**Fees on small amounts**
- Stripe's standard US card rate is 2.9% + $0.30 per charge. On a $3 donation that's about 13%. International cards and currency conversion add more.
- Ko-fi's free plan reportedly charges no platform fee on donations. You still pay the payment processor fees, which are similar to Stripe's. So Ko-fi should cost about the same as Stripe, or a little more.
- Two sources say new Ko-fi accounts may start on a "Contributor" tier with a 5% fee, including on tips. Check your account settings.

**Ko-fi**
- Setup takes minutes, and the page looks like a donation page. Supporters can pick "buy a coffee" amounts, and you can add memberships or a shop later.
- Supporters can use PayPal as well as cards, which can help conversion.
- It has less branding control, and your money passes through Ko-fi's and PayPal's systems.

**Stripe Payment Links**
- You own the customer relationship and the branding, and it works on your own site.
- Fixed-amount links are simple. I couldn't confirm how "customer chooses amount" is set up or priced, so check Stripe's docs.
- You handle tax, receipts, and refunds yourself. Disputes cost $15 each, which is painful on small donations.

**My recommendation:** Use Ko-fi if your donations will mostly be under about $10 and you want it live quickly. Use Stripe if you're a registered business or nonprofit, expect larger or recurring gifts, or need your own branding. Whichever you pick, confirm the current rates on ko-fi.com and stripe.com/pricing. My sources were mostly third-party blogs, and they disagreed in places.
