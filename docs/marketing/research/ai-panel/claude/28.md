# 28: What's the best Patreon alternative for a writer with their own website?

**Ghost** is probably the best fit for a writer who already has a website. Which option is best depends on how much you want to run yourself.

**Best overall: Ghost (Ghost(Pro) or self-hosted)**
- It's a publishing platform with built-in paid memberships and newsletters, so your site and subscriptions live in one place.
- It takes **0% of your revenue**. You only pay Stripe's processing fees plus Ghost's hosting fee, or your own server costs if you self-host.
- You own your domain and your subscriber list, and you can export both.
- Downsides: it's a monthly cost whether or not you earn anything, and it has no built-in discovery network.

**Other options**
- **Substack:** This is the easiest to set up, and it has a discovery network. It takes a 10% cut plus Stripe fees. Your content lives on their platform, though you can use a custom domain. It's best if you want an audience more than you want to own the infrastructure.
- **Buttondown:** This is a lightweight, writer-friendly newsletter tool with paid subscriptions through Stripe. It's cheap, and good if you want to keep your existing site and add a newsletter.
- **WordPress with a membership plugin (MemberPress, Paid Memberships Pro, or WP's built-in Stripe tools):** This is the best choice if your site is already on WordPress. You get full control, but you handle more of the setup and maintenance.
- **Memberful or Supporting Cast:** These add paid memberships to an existing site (WordPress, Ghost, custom) or to podcasts. They charge a small fee on top of Stripe.
- **Ko-fi:** This works for tips and light memberships. It has low or no fees on the free tier, but it's less polished for serialized writing.
- **Stripe Payment Links or Checkout:** If you only need simple recurring support, you can add these directly to your site. There's no platform cut, but you'd have to build the gated content yourself.

**Quick guide**
- You want the most control and the lowest fees: **Ghost**.
- You're already on WordPress: **a membership plugin**.
- You want the least setup and built-in discovery: **Substack**.
- You want a simple newsletter alongside your existing site: **Buttondown**.

Pricing and fee structures change often, so check each platform's current pricing page before committing. If you tell me what your site runs on, whether you write fiction or nonfiction, and whether you serialize chapters, I can narrow this down.
