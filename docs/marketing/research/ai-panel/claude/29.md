# 29: How do I monetize my blog without ads?

Here are the main ways to monetize a blog without ads, roughly ordered from easiest to start to most scalable.

## 1. Sell your own products
- **Digital products:** ebooks, templates, checklists, presets, printables, swipe files. Margins are high and there's no inventory.
- **Courses or workshops:** a good fit if your blog teaches a skill. Cohort-based courses usually command higher prices than self-paced ones.
- **Physical products or merch:** works if you have a strong brand or community.

## 2. Paid memberships and subscriptions
- **Paid newsletter tiers** through Substack, Ghost, Beehiiv, or Buttondown. Offer premium posts, archives, or early access.
- **Community memberships** through Circle, Discord, or Patreon, with exclusive content, Q&As, or a private forum.
- **Memberships on your own site** through Ghost, Memberful, or WordPress plugins.

## 3. Services
- **Consulting or coaching:** your blog works as proof of expertise.
- **Freelancing:** writing, design, development, or whatever your blog showcases.
- **Speaking and workshops:** paid talks for companies or conferences.

## 4. Affiliate marketing (not ads)
- Recommend tools and products you actually use and earn a commission. Done honestly, it reads as a recommendation, not an ad.
- Disclose affiliate links clearly (required by the FTC in the US and similar rules elsewhere).
- It works best in reviews, "best tools for X" guides, and tutorials.

## 5. Sponsorships and partnerships
- **Sponsored posts or newsletter sponsorships:** a flat fee from brands relevant to your audience. This is often more lucrative than programmatic ads.
- **Long-term sponsor deals:** a recurring slot, such as "This month's post is brought to you by…".

## 6. Reader support
- **Tip jars and donations:** Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, or Patreon.
- This works best when you have loyal readers who feel they get a lot of value.

## 7. Content-based revenue
- **Books:** publish a traditional or self-published book built on your blog content.
- **Licensing or syndication:** other publications may pay to republish your work.
- **Job board or directory:** if your niche has an industry, charge employers to post listings.

## How to choose
1. **Start with your audience's needs.** What problems do they keep asking you about? That points to the right product or service.
2. **Build an email list first.** It's the base for almost every method above, and you own it.
3. **Match the method to your size.**
   - Small audience (under 1,000): services, consulting, and a simple digital product.
   - Medium audience: memberships, affiliate links, and sponsorships.
   - Large audience: courses, communities, and premium products.
4. **Diversify.** Combining 2–3 streams is more stable than relying on one.
