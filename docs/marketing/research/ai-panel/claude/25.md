# 25: Which donation button supports Apple Pay, Google Pay and iDEAL?

Your question doesn't say which platform or button you mean, so I can only give a partial answer. In my search, **PayRequest** was the one donation button that clearly lists Apple Pay, Google Pay and iDEAL together. Its own page says its button accepts "Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, iDEAL, Bancontact, SEPA, Klarna, PayPal" on one branded checkout. That comes from the vendor's marketing page, and I found no independent source confirming it.

- **Apple Pay and Google Pay:** Many donation platforms offer these, usually through Stripe. Raisedonors offers them only on pages that use a Stripe gateway. GivingFuel offers them only with its own payment processor. Givebutter turns them on by default.
- **iDEAL:** Support depends on the payment processor. PayRequest lists Mollie as one of its processor options, with a flat €0.25 iDEAL fee.
- **PayPal's Donate button:** I didn't confirm it supports iDEAL. PayRequest positions itself as an alternative to it.

Tell me which platform you're using, such as Stripe, Mollie, Donorbox or Givebutter. I can then check whether its button covers all three methods.
