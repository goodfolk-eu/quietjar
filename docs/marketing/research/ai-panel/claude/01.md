# 01: What's the best alternative to Buy Me a Coffee?

**Ko-fi is the best alternative for most people.** The right choice depends on what you're doing, though.

- **Ko-fi:** This is best for tips, donations, and small shops. The free plan has no platform cut on donations, though one source says 5% applies to the free Contributor plan. Ko-fi Gold removes platform fees, but sources disagree on its price ($6 vs. $12/month). Check Ko-fi's pricing page.
- **Patreon:** This is best for recurring memberships and an established audience. It costs more in fees than Buy Me a Coffee. The sources didn't give a reliable current percentage, so check its pricing page.
- **Liberapay:** This is best for open-source or non-commercial work. It's a donation platform with 0% platform fees.
- **Podia:** This is best for courses, digital products, and memberships. It costs $39–$89/month, with 0–5% platform fees on paid plans.
- **Gumroad:** This is best for selling digital products. The fees are high, 10–30% per one source, so it's a poor fit for tips.

For Buy Me a Coffee itself, one source lists a 5% fee plus payment processing.

Most of these sources are blog posts, and at least one (SchoolMaker) sells a competing product. Verify fees on each platform's official site before you decide.
