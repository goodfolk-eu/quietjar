# 04: What are the best PayPal donate button alternatives?

Here are the main alternatives, grouped by what you need. The fee figures come mostly from vendor blogs, so check each provider's pricing page before you commit.

**Baseline:** PayPal's standard rate is about 2.89% + $0.49 per transaction. Registered charities can get about 1.99% + $0.49. The fixed $0.49 fee hurts most on small donations.

**Donation platforms (forms, recurring gifts, donor management)**
- **Donorbox:** Popular and embeddable. It has a free tier with a platform fee plus processing fees. Its own pages give conflicting numbers (roughly 1.75% on one page, 2.95% on another), so verify them. Donors can optionally cover the fees.
- **Givebutter:** Aimed at nonprofits, with fundraising tools and a tip-based model.
- **Glass Register:** Claims zero platform fees on its free forms. That's a marketing claim, so confirm it.

**Payment processors (more DIY, lower platform cost)**
- **Stripe:** About 2.9% + $0.30. You can use Stripe Payment Links for a simple donate button, or build a custom form on it. Most donation tools run on Stripe anyway.
- **Square:** About 2.9% + $0.30 online. The free Square Online donation page works even if you don't have a website.

**Crowdfunding and creator-style options**
- **GoFundMe:** Good for one-off campaigns. It adds a platform fee on top of processing, so check it separately.
- Tools like Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee and GitHub Sponsors suit individual creators and open-source projects. I didn't research their fees in this search.

**How to choose**
- **Individual or small project:** Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, or a Stripe Payment Link.
- **Registered nonprofit:** Givebutter or Donorbox, or PayPal's discounted charity rate if you want to keep PayPal.
- **Lowest fees and you can build it:** Stripe directly.
- **Many small donations:** Avoid the $0.49 fixed fee by using a processor with a lower fixed fee, or by letting donors cover fees.
