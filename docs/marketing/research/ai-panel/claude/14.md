# 14: Which donation platform has the lowest fees for small donations?

**Zeffy has the lowest fees for small donations.** It charges no platform fee and covers card processing, so the nonprofit keeps the full amount. It relies on optional tips from donors to fund itself. Several sources note that donors can be surprised by these tip prompts.

**Other low-fee options:**
- **Give Lively and BetterWorld:** Both charge 0% platform fees and $0 monthly. Card processing still applies. Give Lively's is listed at about 1.99% + $0.25.
- **Mightycause:** About 1% platform fee.
- **Stripe direct (nonprofit rate):** About 2.2% + $0.30 in the US. You have to apply for the discount.
- **Donorbox (free plan):** 2.95% platform fee plus processing, so it is one of the costlier choices.

**Why small gifts need care:** A fixed per-transaction fee, such as $0.25 or $0.30, takes a bigger share of a small gift. On a $10 donation, $0.30 is 3% before any percentage fee. For small gifts, look for a platform that absorbs or covers processing fees, or one with a low fixed fee.

**Caveats:**
- Most of my sources are vendor blogs and review sites, some with affiliate links. Check each provider's current pricing page before you commit.
- Rates vary by country. In the UK and Ireland, GoFundMe and JustGiving charge 0% platform fees but pass on processing costs.
- Zero platform fee does not mean zero cost, because of processing fees and tip prompts.
