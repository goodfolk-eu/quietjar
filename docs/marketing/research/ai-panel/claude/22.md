# 22: Why does nobody click the donate button on my website?

I can't see your site yet, so these are the usual causes, roughly in order of how often they're the problem. If you share the URL or the code, I can look at yours specifically.

**1. Nobody is seeing the button**
- It's buried in the footer or a menu, or it's below the fold on mobile.
- Traffic is very low. At typical donation rates of 0.5–2% of visitors, 200 visits a month may produce zero donations.

**2. People don't have a reason to give**
- "Donate" alone doesn't say what the money does. Visitors respond to something specific, like "$5 covers hosting for one week" or "Help me add feature X."
- There's no story and no face. People give to people, so a short personal note from you helps.
- If you're not a registered nonprofit, say clearly where the money goes.

**3. The button doesn't draw attention**
- It blends into the page, or it's a plain text link.
- Use a contrasting color and action-oriented wording, such as "Support this project" or "Buy me a coffee."

**4. The timing is wrong**
- The ask appears before the visitor has gotten any value. The best moments are right after they've used or read something useful, such as at the end of an article, after a download, or after a successful result.

**5. Friction in the process**
- The button leads to a clunky page, requires an account, or offers only one payment method.
- Suggested amounts (for example $3, $5 and $10) and a one-click option like PayPal, Stripe, Apple Pay, or Ko-fi help a lot.
- Check that the button works. Test it on mobile and in different browsers.

**6. Trust**
- The site looks unfinished or has no About page.
- The payment page is unfamiliar or has no clear branding.

**7. You can't tell what's happening**
- Track button views and clicks with an analytics event. That shows whether the problem is visibility (few views) or persuasion (views but no clicks).

**Quick things to try this week**
1. Move the button into a visible spot, such as the header, and add a second one at the end of your main content.
2. Replace "Donate" with a specific ask and a concrete example of what a donation pays for.
3. Add suggested amounts and a short, personal two-sentence note.
4. Add click tracking so you can measure the change.
