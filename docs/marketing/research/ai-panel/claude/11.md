# 11: Can I use Stripe to accept donations on my personal website?

Yes, but it depends on how you frame it. Stripe's rules make this a gray area for individuals.

- **Tips and donations:** Stripe's rules say a tip must be for a good or service you provided. A donation must go to a specific charitable purpose. A general "support my site" button may not clearly fit either.
- **Personal accounts:** You can open a Stripe account as an Individual / Sole Proprietor. Stripe doesn't support personal or peer-to-peer money transfers, though.
- **Nonprofits:** Fundraising for registered nonprofits, charities and similar organizations is generally accepted if you can document their status. One user on Reddit, whose account was denied, reported this. That's anecdotal, and Stripe often doesn't explain denials.

**What to do:**
1. Read Stripe's [tips and donations requirements](https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-for-accepting-tips-or-donations) and its Prohibited and Restricted Businesses list.
2. If your use is a gray area, ask Stripe support before you launch. This avoids having funds frozen later.
3. If you want something low-risk, consider offering something in return, such as content, a digital download or a membership. Platforms built for creators, like Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors or Patreon, are also designed for tips from individuals.
