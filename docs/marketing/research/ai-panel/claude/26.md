# 26: What's the fastest way for a visitor to donate on my website without creating an account?

Fastest option: **a hosted donation link or button with guest checkout, with Apple Pay and Google Pay turned on.** Visitors can give in two or three taps, with no account and no typing of card details.

**Quickest ways to set it up (about 10 minutes each)**
1. **Stripe Payment Links.** Create a link in the Stripe dashboard and choose "Customers choose what to pay". Then add a button on your site that points to it. Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link are included automatically, and there are no accounts.
2. **PayPal Donate button.** Visitors can pay as a guest by card, or use PayPal, Venmo or Apple Pay where available. It's easy to set up, but it sends visitors to PayPal's page.
3. **Donation platforms** such as Givebutter, Zeffy, Donorbox or Classy. They are built for donations and include receipts, recurring gifts and donor management. Some are free and rely on optional tips from donors.

**What makes it fast**
- **Preset amounts** (for example $10 / $25 / $50 / other), with one preselected.
- **Wallet payments** (Apple Pay, Google Pay) at the top of the form.
- **Only the essentials.** Ask for the amount and payment, plus an email for the receipt. Make everything else optional.
- **A prominent button** in your header and on the homepage, linking straight to the donation form.
- **Mobile-first layout.** Most donors will be on a phone.
- **An embedded or overlay form** so visitors don't leave your site. A hosted page is fine if it loads fast.
- **A "make it monthly" checkbox** instead of a separate flow.

**Caveats**
- If you're a registered nonprofit, use a donation-specific platform so you can issue tax receipts. Stripe and PayPal can do this, but you'll handle more of the setup yourself.
- Compare fees. Processing is typically around 2.9% + $0.30, and platforms may add their own fees or tip prompts.
