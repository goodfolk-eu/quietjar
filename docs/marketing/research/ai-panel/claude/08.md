# 08: How do I accept tips on my Ghost blog?

Ghost has a built-in Tips & Donations feature.

1. **Connect Stripe.** Your publication needs an active Stripe connection.
2. **Open the settings.** In Ghost Admin, go to **Settings → Growth → Tips & Donations**.
3. **Configure it.** Set a suggested amount and a currency.
4. **Share the link.** Ghost gives you a payment link. You can put it in a button, a header, an email call to action, or your site navigation.

Both members and non-members can tip, and you get an email notification for each payment. Ghost(Pro) sites can use it right away. Self-hosted sites need to be on a recent version.

I didn't find anything on current fees or limits. Stripe's standard processing fees will apply, so check Ghost's help page for details.
