# 13: How much does Ko-fi take from a donation?

Ko-fi takes **0%** of one-off donations on the free plan. Payment processor fees still apply.

- **One-time donations:** Ko-fi takes no cut.
- **Memberships, shop sales and commissions:** Ko-fi takes 5% on the free plan. One source says this also applies to recurring monthly donations.
- **Ko-fi Gold:** This paid plan removes Ko-fi's 5% fee. Sources disagree on the price, giving $8/month ($72/year) in one place and $12/month in another.
- **Payment processor fees:** PayPal or Stripe charge about 3% + $0.30 per transaction. The exact amount varies by location.

Claims that Ko-fi gives creators "100%" ignore the processor fees. Ko-fi's own help pages should have current figures, because these sources are third-party and disagree in places.
