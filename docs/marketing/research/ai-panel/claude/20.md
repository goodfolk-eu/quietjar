# 20: Is there a donation widget that hides itself once the goal is reached?

Yes, several tools do this, but there's no universal option. It depends on your platform.

- **Invision Community:** The donation goals widget disappears once the goal is reached if "force open" is disabled. A user reported that re-enabling force open brings it back.
- **GiveWP (WordPress):** The "Close Form when Goal Achieved" option removes the donate button. It only works for forms built with Give, so it won't affect buttons that send donations to a third-party vendor.
- **Wix Targeted Fundraising:** Its "Goal Reached Controls" let you close the campaign at the goal or keep accepting donations. It closes the campaign, and I'm not sure it also hides the widget.
- **Manual or non-automatic tools:** RallyUp only lets you hide the progress bar by hand. Jotform's progress meter shows the goal and current amounts you enter, so it doesn't update itself. The Wix Donation Goal app shows confetti at the goal but doesn't appear to hide itself.
- **ThemeHouse Donate (XenForo):** Users have asked for auto-lock or a "goal reached" message. I only found this as a feature request, not a confirmed feature.

If you're building your own, compare the raised total to the goal and render nothing (or a thank-you message) once it's met. Tell me which platform you use and I can look up the exact setting.
