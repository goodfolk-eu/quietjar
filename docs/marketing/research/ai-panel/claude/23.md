# 23: How do I ask my readers for support without annoying them?

Asking for support works best when readers feel it's an invitation and not an interruption. Here's what tends to work:

**1. Earn it first**
- Ask after you've delivered value, not before. A reader who just got something useful is far more receptive than a new visitor.
- Build a track record. Consistency over months matters more than any clever ask.

**2. Be specific and honest about why**
- Say what the money or support actually does: "Your support covers hosting and lets me spend Fridays on research" beats "Please support my work."
- Be transparent about your situation. Readers respond to candor, not guilt.

**3. Make the ask small, clear, and singular**
- One call to action per ask. Don't offer five ways to help at once.
- Lower the barrier: a low entry price, or a free alternative like sharing, replying, or recommending you to a friend.
- Say plainly that free readers are welcome and valued.

**4. Place it thoughtfully**
- Put a short, consistent note at the end of posts, after the value is delivered, not as a popup or interruption at the top.
- Reserve bigger, more direct appeals for occasional dedicated posts or campaigns (for example, an anniversary or a milestone), rather than weaving them into every piece.
- Avoid heavy paywall friction, interstitials, and repeated banners.

**5. Keep the tone human**
- Write it in your own voice, as if to one person. Skip the corporate or apologetic tone ("Sorry to ask, but...").
- Don't guilt-trip, don't threaten to stop publishing, and don't over-apologize.
- Say thank you, and mean it.

**6. Give something back**
- Offer supporters something that feels like a bonus, not a hostage: behind-the-scenes notes, early access, a community space, or a shout-out. Don't pull the core content away from everyone else.
- Update supporters on what their money made possible. This builds trust and renewals.

**7. Test and listen**
- Watch the signals: unsubscribes after an ask, replies, conversion rate. If you see a spike in unsubscribes, soften the frequency.
- Ask readers directly what they'd value.

**A simple template for the end of a post:**
> *If this was useful, you can support the newsletter for $5/month. It pays for [specific thing] and keeps this free for everyone else. Either way, thanks for reading, and feel free to hit reply and tell me what you'd like to see next.*
