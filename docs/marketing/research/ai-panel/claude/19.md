# 19: How do I cover my website's monthly hosting bill with donations?

# Covering your hosting bill with donations

## 1. Pick a platform
| Platform | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ko-fi** | One-off "buy me a coffee" tips | Charges 0% on one-off donations (you only pay payment-processor fees). Easy to set up. |
| **Buy Me a Coffee** | Similar to Ko-fi | Takes a platform fee, and also supports memberships. |
| **GitHub Sponsors** | Open-source or developer projects | Charges no fee on individual sponsorships. |
| **Liberapay** | Recurring donations, privacy-minded audiences | Nonprofit, and doesn't take a cut. |
| **Patreon** | Ongoing memberships with perks | Takes a percentage, and is best if you produce regular content. |
| **Open Collective** | Transparent, community-run projects | Public ledger, and can act as a fiscal host. |
| **PayPal.me / Stripe Payment Links** | Full control | Simple, but you handle the presentation yourself. |

Check each platform's current fees, because they change often.

## 2. Make the goal concrete
- Say exactly what the money pays for, for example "Hosting costs $24/month".
- Show a progress bar or a line such as "Covered 70% this month". Visible, specific goals convert much better than a vague "support me".
- Be transparent. If you publish costs and say what happens to any surplus, people trust you more.

## 3. Placement matters
- Put a small, non-intrusive link in the footer and on an "About" or "Support" page.
- Add a soft call-to-action at the end of your most useful pages or posts, where readers have just got value.
- Mention it in your newsletter or release notes.
- If it's open source, add a `FUNDING.yml` file to the repo and a badge in the README.

## 4. Encourage recurring support
Monthly donations match a monthly bill. Offer a few tiers, such as $3, $5 and $10 a month. Even 10–20 small recurring donors can cover a typical hosting bill.

## 5. Practical points
- **Traffic reality:** Typically only about 0.5–2% of regular visitors donate. Covering a $20–50 bill usually takes a few thousand monthly visitors with an engaged audience.
- **Taxes:** Donations are generally taxable income in most countries, so keep records. A tip is not the same as a tax-deductible charitable donation, which usually requires nonprofit status.
- **Say thanks:** Add a thank-you page or a supporters list (with permission). This improves retention.
- **Reduce the cost:** Static hosting (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, GitHub Pages) is often free or nearly free, which can shrink the bill.

## 6. Alternatives
Light, privacy-friendly ads or affiliate links, or sponsorships from relevant companies, can supplement donations if they alone fall short.
