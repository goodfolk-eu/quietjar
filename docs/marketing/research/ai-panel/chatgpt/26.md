# 26: What's the fastest way for a visitor to donate on my website without creating an account?

The fastest way is to add a Donate Now button that takes visitors directly to a secure payment page, where they can donate immediately without registering or creating an account.

Ideally, the visitor should be able to:

Click Donate Now.

Choose a donation amount (or enter their own).

Pay using a credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or another available payment method.

Receive an automatic donation confirmation.

Don't force visitors to sign up, log in, or complete a lengthy form. Every extra step can reduce donations.
