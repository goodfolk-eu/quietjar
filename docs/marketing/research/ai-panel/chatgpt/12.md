# 12: Should I use Stripe Payment Links or Ko-fi for small donations?

For small, one-off donations, I'd generally recommend Ko-fi, unless you specifically want a payment page integrated into your own website or brand.

The biggest difference isn't just fees: it's the experience you're offering donors.

## Ko-fi vs Stripe Payment Links

Ko-fi

Best for simple donations

Ready-made donation page.

Suggested donation amounts and custom amounts.

Supporter messages and optional recurring support.

Can connect Stripe or PayPal.

0% Ko-fi platform fee on one-off tips when using its free mode.

Normal payment processing fees still apply.

Ko-fi Help

+1

Stripe Payment Links

Best for control

Direct payment links without coding.

More control over branding and checkout.

Supports one-time payments and subscriptions.

No additional Payment Links fee on standard Stripe pricing.

More flexibility if you later build your own donation system.

Standard Stripe processing fees apply.

## What about fees?

If you're based in the Netherlands, Stripe's published standard EEA card rate is 1.5% + €0.25 per successful transaction. iDEAL payments are €0.29.

This is why small donations can be disproportionately expensive: the fixed transaction fee matters quite a bit.

Ko-fi's free mode adds no platform fee to one-off tips, but the underlying Stripe or PayPal processing fee remains.

What kind of donations are you collecting?

B. Open-source project — people support your work.

C. Website/project donations — visitors contribute small amounts.

D. Nonprofit/charitable donations.
