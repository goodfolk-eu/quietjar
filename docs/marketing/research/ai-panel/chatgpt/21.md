# 21: Does a donate button on a website actually make money?

Yes! A Donate button on a website can absolutely make money, but it depends on the website, its visitors, and how compelling the reason to donate is.

The interesting thing is that you don't necessarily need to be a charity to receive donations.

## How it works

When someone visits your website, they might see a button such as:

# ❤️ Support This Website

If you find this website useful, consider making a small donation to help keep it running.

☕ Donate €5

Example of a donation button

Visitors can donate through services such as:

PayPal – donation buttons and payment links.

Ko-fi – people can buy you a coffee.

Buy Me a Coffee – one-time or recurring support.

Stripe – accept payments directly.

## One interesting idea

You could create a website that offers something genuinely useful for free, then add a donation button.

For example:

A free online calculator.

A free PDF converter.

A free image compression tool.

A free AI-powered utility.

A free collection of useful resources.

People use the tool for free, and some voluntarily contribute €2–€10.

The key is traffic + usefulness + trust.
