# 11: Can I use Stripe to accept donations on my personal website?

Yes! You can use Stripe to accept donations directly on your personal website, even if you aren't running a registered charity or nonprofit.

Stripe supports one-time payments and recurring contributions, so you can let visitors support your work, project, website, or personal cause.

There are a few ways to set it up, depending on how much customization you want.

## 3 ways to accept donations

Stripe Payment Links (easiest) – Create a donation link in Stripe and add a button to your website. No coding required.

Embedded Stripe Checkout – Add a more integrated payment experience to your website.

Custom donation form – Build your own interface with suggested amounts, custom amounts and recurring payment options.

Stripe supports one-time and recurring fixed-amount contributions. Donors can also choose their own amount for one-time payments.

## Important: Stripe's donation rules

There is one important distinction:

Tips: You can accept tips for content or services you've provided.

Donations: These must support a specific charitable purpose that you commit to carrying out.

Personal gifts: Stripe does not support using its payment processing for personal or peer-to-peer money transfers.
