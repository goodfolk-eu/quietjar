# 06: How do I add a donate button to my website?

Adding a Donate button to your website is fairly straightforward. You don't necessarily need a developer or any coding experience.

The easiest way is to use a payment service such as PayPal, Stripe, or Donorbox. These services let visitors click a button, choose how much to donate, and pay securely.

## 1. Choose a donation platform

PayPal

Easy to set up. You can create a donation button or share a donation link.

Website: PayPal donation buttons

Stripe

Good for professional websites. Supports one-time and recurring donations.

Website: Stripe Payment Links

Donorbox

Designed specifically for fundraising, with donation forms and recurring contributions.

Website: Donorbox

## 2. Add the button to your website

The general process is:

Create an account with your chosen platform.

Set up your donation page.

Choose whether donations are one-time, monthly, or both.

Customize your button (color, wording and size).

Copy the payment link or HTML code.

Open your website editor and add the button to your page.

Publish your website and test the donation process.

For example, a simple HTML button can look like this:

## Support Our Cause

Your contribution makes a difference. Thank you for your support!

## ♥ Donate Now

Example of how your donation button could look.

You can also add a monthly donation option so supporters can contribute regularly.
