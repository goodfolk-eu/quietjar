# 19: How do I cover my website's monthly hosting bill with donations?

You can cover your website's monthly hosting bill through donations by setting up a small “Support this website” system that lets visitors contribute voluntarily.

The simplest approach is to make your hosting costs transparent, provide an easy way to donate, and encourage recurring monthly contributions.

Here's how I'd set it up.

## 1. Choose a donation platform

These are good options depending on how you want to receive donations:

Ko-fi

Great for a simple donation button. Visitors can make one-time contributions or monthly memberships.

I'd recommend offering small amounts such as €1, €3, €5 and €10.

You don't need hundreds of donors. A handful of regular supporters can cover basic hosting.

## 3. Add a donation section to your website

I'd suggest placing a small section in your footer and perhaps a more visible link in your navigation.

Here's example wording you can use:

Help keep this website online!

This website is supported by its visitors. Your donations help pay for hosting, maintenance and other running costs.

Replace the example amount and button destination with your actual details.

## 4. Encourage recurring donations

This is particularly important because hosting bills arrive every month.

For example:

€1/month — Friend of the website

€3/month — Supporter

€5/month — Website supporter

€10/month — Premium supporter

You can offer recognition or a small thank-you, but don't necessarily need to provide exclusive content.

## 5. Be transparent

Tell visitors:

How much hosting costs.

What donations pay for.

Whether donations are recurring or one-time.

What happens if you collect more than the hosting bill.

Also remember that donations can have tax implications, particularly if you operate the website from the Netherlands.

One useful detail: Ko-fi's fees vary by payment type. One-time tips can have a 0% platform fee on its free mode, while monthly tips and memberships have a 5% platform fee, plus payment processing fees.
