# 29: How do I monetize my blog without ads?

You can monetize a blog without displaying a single advertisement. In fact, many blogs make more money through products, services, affiliate partnerships, and paid content than they would through traditional display ads.

The key is to turn your blog from a place where people simply read content into a business that provides value and solves problems.

Here are 8 effective ways to monetize your blog without ads.

## 1. Affiliate marketing

Great for beginners

Recommend products or services that are relevant to your readers and earn a commission when someone makes a purchase through your referral link.

For example:

A travel blog can recommend hotels, luggage, and travel insurance.

A food blog can recommend kitchen equipment and cookbooks.

A lifestyle blog can recommend skincare and home products.

A finance blog can recommend budgeting tools and software.

Affiliate networks and programs to explore:

Awin

Amazon Associates

Impact

PartnerStack

Tip: Focus on products you genuinely recommend. Helpful product comparisons and tutorials often work better than random affiliate links.

## 2. Sell digital products

High profit potential

Create something once and sell it repeatedly without worrying about inventory or shipping.

Printable planners

€5–€20

E-books and guides

€9–€39

Templates

€7–€49

Mini-courses

€19–€99+

For example, if you run a personal finance blog, you could sell:

A monthly budget spreadsheet.

A debt repayment planner.

A savings challenge.

A personal finance e-book.

Platforms to consider: Gumroad and Payhip.

## 3. Offer paid memberships

Build a community around your blog and charge readers a recurring subscription.

You could offer:

Exclusive articles.

Premium tutorials.

Downloadable resources.

Private community access.

Monthly Q&A sessions.

Behind-the-scenes content.

For example, 100 members paying €8 per month generate €800 in monthly recurring revenue before fees and expenses.

Platforms: Patreon and Ghost.

## 4. Offer freelance services or consulting

Your blog can act as your portfolio and demonstrate your expertise.

Depending on your niche, you could offer:

Writing and editing.

SEO consulting.

Graphic design.

Coaching.

Business consulting.

Photography.

Website design.

This can be particularly profitable because you don't need thousands of visitors to find paying clients.

## 5. Create sponsored content (without display ads)

You can work directly with brands without placing banner advertisements on your website.

Examples:

A sponsored in-depth article.

A product review.

A brand collaboration.

A sponsored newsletter.

A tutorial featuring a company's product.

You negotiate a fixed fee rather than relying on ad impressions or clicks.

Make sure sponsored content is clearly disclosed to readers.

## 6. Sell online courses and workshops

If your blog teaches people something valuable, turn your knowledge into a structured learning experience.

Examples:

Cooking blog → Online cooking classes.

Photography blog → Photography course.

Marketing blog → SEO training.

Fitness blog → Workout programs.

Parenting blog → Practical parenting workshops.

Platforms include Teachable and Thinkific.

## 7. Monetize your email newsletter

Build an email list through your blog and create a direct relationship with your audience.

You can monetize it through:

Paid newsletter subscriptions.

Affiliate recommendations.

Your own digital products.

Exclusive educational content.

Paid workshops.

The advantage is that you aren't relying entirely on search engine traffic or social media algorithms.

## 8. Sell physical products

If your audience is passionate about your niche, consider selling products related to your content.

Examples:

Food blog → Recipe books and kitchen accessories.

Gardening blog → Seed kits and gardening planners.

Art blog → Prints and merchandise.

Travel blog → Travel journals and accessories.

You can start with print-on-demand or small product collections rather than holding lots of inventory.

## A practical 30-day starting plan

Week 1: Identify your audience's biggest problems and review your most popular articles.

Week 2: Choose one monetization method and create a simple offer.

Week 3: Set up an email subscription and a sales page.

Week 4: Promote your offer through relevant blog posts and your newsletter. Track clicks, subscribers and sales.

Don't spend months creating products before checking whether people actually want them. Test demand first.
