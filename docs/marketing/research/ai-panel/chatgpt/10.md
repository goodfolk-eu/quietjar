# 10: Is there an embeddable donation widget I can put on any website?

Yes! There are several embeddable donation widgets that you can add to almost any website, even if you didn't build the website yourself.

Typically, they work by giving you a snippet of HTML/JavaScript or an iframe that you paste into your website. The widget can let visitors:

Make one-time donations.

Set up recurring monthly or yearly donations.

Choose their own donation amount.

Pay by credit card or other supported payment methods.

Receive donation confirmations and receipts.

Works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and more.

5. Dotatie

Designed for Dutch foundations and associations.

iDEAL/Wero, SEPA direct debit and cards.

Recurring donations.

Embeddable donation forms.

Dotatie WordPress plugin

## My recommendation

If you want something that works on virtually any website, I'd start with Donorbox.

If you're targeting Dutch or European donors, I'd look closely at Donly or Dotatie because of iDEAL/Wero and Mollie integration.

## One question to narrow it down

Are you building this for:

Your own organisation – you want to collect donations on your website.

Multiple organisations – you want to offer a donation widget that other website owners can embed on their websites.

A public donation platform – anyone can create a donation campaign and embed their own widget anywhere.

The distinction matters quite a bit, especially if you want to build a reusable donation widget as a product.
