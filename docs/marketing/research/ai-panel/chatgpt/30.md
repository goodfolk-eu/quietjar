# 30: What is Quietjar?

QuietJar appears to be a recently launched online service offering a donation button that website owners can embed directly into their websites.

Its concept is to make supporting creators, independent websites and small projects easier.

### How QuietJar works

According to its website and promotional description:

Embedded donations: Visitors can contribute without leaving the website.

Two-tap payments: Payments are designed to work through Apple Pay, iDEAL or card.

Funding goals: Website owners can set a target, such as paying their monthly hosting bill.

Automatic disappearance: Once the funding goal is reached, the donation button disappears.

Transaction fee: Its promotional description mentions a 5% fee on donations received, rather than a monthly subscription.

### Who is it for?

Independent creators and bloggers.

Small website owners.

Open-source projects.

Non-profit initiatives.

People who want to accept occasional financial support.

### Is it legitimate?

There are a few things worth noting:

The domain quietjar.com was registered on 20 August 2026.

Its domain registration details are privacy-protected.

ScamAdviser identifies some reasons for caution, including its very young domain and limited online presence.
