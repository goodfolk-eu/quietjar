# 08: How do I accept tips on my Ghost blog?

Ghost has a built-in Tips & Donations feature that lets readers send you one-off payments without subscribing or creating an account.

You can set it up directly in Ghost using Stripe. Here's how.

## 1. Connect Stripe to Ghost

ghost.org

If you haven't connected Stripe yet:

Log in to your Ghost Admin.

Go to Settings → Memberships → Tiers (or Payments, depending on your version).

Click Connect with Stripe.

Sign in to your Stripe account and authorize the connection.

See: Ghost's Stripe setup guide .

## 2. Enable Tips & Donations

ghost.org

In Ghost Admin, go to Settings → Growth → Tips & Donations.

Configure your suggested tip amount.

Choose your preferred currency.

Save your settings.

Ghost will generate a dedicated payment link for your publication.

See: Official Ghost Tips & Donations guide .

## 3. Add a tip button to your blog

You can share your payment link in several places:

At the end of articles: "Enjoyed this article? Buy me a coffee!"

In your navigation: Add a Support My Work link.

In your sidebar or footer: Include a permanent tip link.

In your newsletters: Invite readers to support your writing.

For example:

## Enjoying the blog?

If you find my articles useful, consider supporting my work with a small tip. Every contribution helps me keep creating.

Buy me a coffee

Support independent writing

Illustrative button — replace it with your actual Ghost payment link.

## 4. What happens when someone tips?

Readers can tip without becoming members.

Payments are processed through Stripe.

You receive an email notification when someone tips.

You receive the money through your connected Stripe account.

## What if you don't want to use Stripe?

You can also use an external tipping service such as:

Ko-fi.

Buy Me a Coffee.

PayPal.

Simply add your external payment link to a button or text link on your Ghost site. Ghost also documents external payment integrations: Alternative payment gateways .
