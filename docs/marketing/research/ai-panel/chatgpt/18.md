# 18: How do I show a donation goal with a progress bar on my website?

You can show a donation goal with a visual progress bar on your website using a simple HTML, CSS, and JavaScript component.

For example, it could look like this:

Our fundraising goal

Fundraising live

# €7,500

raised of €10,000 goal

75% funded

€2,500 remaining

Donate now

## Option 1: Simple HTML/CSS progress bar

You can copy this code directly into your website:

## Option 2: Automatically update the progress

If you want the progress bar to update whenever someone donates, you need to connect it to your donation platform or database.

For example:

A visitor makes a donation through Stripe, PayPal, or another payment provider.

Your payment provider confirms the successful payment.

Your website updates the total amount raised.

The progress bar automatically recalculates the percentage.

The formula is:

Progress=Amount raisedDonation goal×100\text{Progress} = \frac{\text{Amount raised}}{\text{Donation goal}}\times100Progress=Donation goalAmount raised​×100
