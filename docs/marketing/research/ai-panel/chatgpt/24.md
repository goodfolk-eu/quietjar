# 24: One-time donations or monthly memberships, which works better for a small creator?

For a small creator, monthly memberships are generally better for long-term income, while one-time donations are better for getting started.

My recommendation is to offer both, but make one-time donations the easiest entry point and memberships the way to support you regularly.

## 1. One-time donations

Best for starting out

medium.com

Advantages:

People can support you without committing.

No pressure to produce exclusive content every month.

Great for people who appreciate your work but have limited budgets.

Easier to promote through social media, blogs, videos or newsletters.

Disadvantages:

Income can fluctuate significantly.

Supporters might donate once and never return.

Harder to plan your finances.

Example:

20 people donate €5 = €100.

10 people donate €10 = €100.

5 people donate €20 = €100.

## 2. Monthly memberships

Best for long-term stability

erikathegoober.com

Advantages:

Predictable monthly income.

Builds a loyal community.

Supporters feel more connected to your work.

Income can grow as your audience grows.

Disadvantages:

People need a reason to keep subscribing.

You may feel pressure to deliver something regularly.

Subscribers can cancel anytime.

Managing membership perks takes time.

## 3. What I'd recommend for a small creator

Start with a simple three-level approach:

☕ One-time support

# €3–€10

For people who want to say thanks.

🌱 Supporter membership

# €3/month

For people who want to support your work regularly.

⭐ Premium membership

# €7/month

For people who want occasional extras, behind-the-scenes content or early access.

One important thing: don't create too many membership perks. You don't want your monetization system to become another full-time job.

## 4. The most important consideration

Your type of content matters:

Art, illustrations, photography: Both work well.

Educational content: Memberships can work particularly well.

Blogging and writing: Both work well.

Free resources, open-source projects: One-time donations can be excellent.

Entertainment and personal content: Memberships can build a strong fan community.

## My verdict

If you're a small creator without a huge audience, I'd start with one-time donations + one affordable monthly membership.

This lets people support you at whatever level they're comfortable with, without making everyone feel obligated to subscribe.

One question that would help me give you a more precise recommendation:

What kind of creator are you?

Art, illustration or photography

Writing, blogging or newsletters

Something else

The best monetization model can be quite different depending on what you create.
