# 20: Is there a donation widget that hides itself once the goal is reached?

Yes! There are donation widgets that can automatically hide themselves once the fundraising target is reached.

A few options worth looking at:

Donorbox – Offers embeddable donation progress meters. You may need custom code or automation to hide the widget when the goal is reached.

Donorbox

Givebutter – Supports fundraising goals and embeddable goal-bar widgets, but reaching the goal doesn't automatically close the campaign.

Givebutter Help Center

Donatty – Offers customizable fundraising widgets, particularly for livestreams.
