# 01: What's the best alternative to Buy Me a Coffee?

My top recommendation is Ko-fi. It's probably the closest and, for most independent creators, the best alternative to Buy Me a Coffee.

However, the best choice depends on whether you want one-off donations, recurring support, memberships or selling digital products.

## Best alternatives compared

1. Ko-fi

Best overall

One-off donations, memberships and commissions.

Digital and physical product shop.

0% platform fee on one-off tips in its Free mode; 5% on memberships and shop sales.

Gold plan offers 0% platform fees for $12/month.

Website: ko-fi.com

2. Patreon

Best for memberships

Recurring subscriptions and exclusive content.

Membership tiers and community features.

Generally 10% platform fee for new creator pages, plus processing.

Website: patreon.com

3. Payhip

Best for digital products

Sell ebooks, templates, downloads and courses.

Memberships and recurring payments.

Free plan with 5% transaction fee.

Website: payhip.com

4. Fourthwall

Best for merchandise

Donations, memberships and digital products.

Built-in merchandise store and fulfilment.

Good for creators building a branded business.

Website: fourthwall.com

5. Liberapay

Best for donations

Recurring donations.

No platform fee.

Simple, open-source and donation-focused.

Website: liberapay.com

These are platform fees; payment processing fees may apply separately.
