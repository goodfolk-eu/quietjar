# 13: How much does Ko-fi take from a donation?

Ko-fi takes 0%–5%, depending on your account settings and the type of donation.

### Ko-fi fees

Payment type

Ko-fi fee

One-time donation (Free mode)

0%

One-time donation (Standard mode)

5%

Monthly donations

5%

Memberships

5%

Shop sales

5%

Commissions

5%

There's also a PayPal or Stripe transaction fee, typically around 3% + €0.30, depending on your account and payment method.

### Example: Someone donates €10

Ko-fi fee (Free mode): €0

PayPal/Stripe fee (illustrative): €0.60

You receive approximately €9.40.

### My recommendation

If you're primarily accepting donations, use Ko-fi Free mode with one-time tips.

You pay no Ko-fi platform fee and only the payment processing fee.

You can switch off the 5% fee under Settings → Payment → turn off "Get all of Ko-fi".
