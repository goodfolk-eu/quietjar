# 16: Is GitHub Sponsors worth it for a small open source project?

Yes — GitHub Sponsors is worth setting up for a small open-source project, but I wouldn't treat it as a serious revenue stream initially.

My recommendation is to enable it early, keep it simple, and let it grow organically.

The biggest advantage is that it gives people who already use and appreciate your project an easy way to support its development. You don't need thousands of users to justify having a sponsorship option.

## When GitHub Sponsors is worth it

You have regular users: Even a small group of users can generate recurring support.

Your project solves a real problem: Developers and companies may be willing to support something they depend on.

You maintain it regularly: Sponsors are more likely to contribute if they see ongoing development.

You want to make maintenance sustainable: Even €20–100 per month can help cover hosting, infrastructure, or development expenses.

## How I'd set it up

Keep the tiers simple. For example:

Business

$50/month

Company recognition

I'd also enable one-time contributions. Some people prefer making a single donation rather than subscribing.

A few important points:

Don't promise priority support unless you genuinely want to provide it.

Don't put essential features behind sponsorship tiers.

Don't spend too much time designing rewards.

Explain what sponsorship money will fund.

GitHub allows up to 10 monthly and 10 one-time tiers. Personal-account sponsorships have no GitHub fees; organization-account sponsorships can incur fees of up to 6%.

## How to get your first sponsors

Add a Sponsor button to your repository.

Include a short funding section in your README.

Explain why maintaining the project takes time.

Thank contributors and sponsors publicly (with their permission).

Mention sponsorship occasionally in release announcements.

If companies use your project commercially, make it easy for them to sponsor it.

You can enable the repository button using a .github/FUNDING.yml file.

## My verdict

Worth doing

I'd spend 30–60 minutes setting it up and then largely forget about it while continuing to develop the project.

The important thing is that sponsorship is a way to monetize existing value, not a way to create an audience.

The best sponsorship strategy differs quite a bit depending on whether your users are individual developers or businesses.
