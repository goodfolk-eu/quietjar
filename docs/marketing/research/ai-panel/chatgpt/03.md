# 03: Is there a European alternative to Buy Me a Coffee or Patreon?

Yes! There are several European alternatives to Patreon and Buy Me a Coffee, including some based in the Netherlands.

If your priority is supporting European businesses, keeping payments within Europe, or having a more GDPR-conscious platform, these are worth considering.

## Best European alternatives

1. Petje.af

Netherlands

Probably the closest Dutch alternative to Patreon and Buy Me a Coffee.

One-time donations and monthly memberships.

Designed for creators, podcasters and independent publishers.

You connect your own payment provider, such as Mollie.

Website: petje.af

2. Tipeee

France

A close competitor to Buy Me a Coffee.

One-off tips and recurring support.

Memberships and exclusive content.

Around 8% platform commission according to its current comparison listing.

Website: tipeee.com

3. Steady

Germany

Particularly good for independent creators and publishers.

Monthly memberships.

Exclusive posts and content.

Paid newsletters.

Supports SEPA direct debit.

Website: steadyhq.com

4. Liberapay

France

Best if you want a donation-based model.

Recurring donations.

No platform commission.

Open-source and non-profit.

No Patreon-style gated membership tiers.

Website: liberapay.com

5. Ghost

United Kingdom

More of a publishing platform than a tipping service.

Paid memberships and subscriptions.

Exclusive articles and newsletters.

You own your website and audience.

No revenue share on memberships.

Website: ghost.org

6. Patronite

Poland

A Patreon-style membership platform.

Recurring financial support.

One-off fundraising.

Membership benefits and exclusive content.

Website: patronite.pl

## My recommendation

If I were narrowing this down:

Closest to Buy Me a Coffee: Petje.af or Tipeee.

Closest to Patreon: Steady or Patronite.

Best for avoiding platform commissions: Liberapay.

Best for owning your audience and publishing platform: Ghost.

One important distinction: European company ownership doesn't automatically mean all payment processing and data storage happen in Europe. For example, some of these platforms still use Stripe or PayPal.

## One question

Are you looking for an alternative primarily for:

A. Receiving one-time donations (like Buy Me a Coffee).

B. Monthly memberships and exclusive content (like Patreon).

C. Both, ideally on one simple creator page.

I can narrow this down to the best options that are genuinely EU-based, preferably with European payment processing such as Mollie or Adyen.
