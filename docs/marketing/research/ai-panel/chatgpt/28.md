# 28: What's the best Patreon alternative for a writer with their own website?

For a writer who already has their own website, my top recommendation is Ghost , particularly if you want to build a paid membership, publish exclusive writing, and retain control over your audience and brand.

The key distinction from Patreon is that you don't necessarily want another platform to become the centre of your publishing business. You want a membership and payment system that works alongside your existing website.

There are three particularly good alternatives, depending on how much control you want.

Best overall for writers

## My recommendation

I'd narrow it down to two:

Ghost if you want to develop a proper paid writing publication, with a newsletter, archive and membership tiers.

Memberful if you already have a website you're happy with and simply want to add a subscription system.

One important consideration: neither Patreon nor these alternatives automatically brings you an audience. Your website, mailing list and relationships with readers remain your main assets.
