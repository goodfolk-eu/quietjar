# Ko-fi vs Buy Me a Coffee: which is better?

My recommendation: Ko-fi is better for most independent creators, especially if you want to receive donations, sell digital products, offer memberships, or earn money from your audience.

Buy Me a Coffee is a good alternative if you want something extremely simple with a polished, straightforward tipping experience.

Here's how they compare as of November 2026.
