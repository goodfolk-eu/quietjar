# 04: What are the best PayPal donate button alternatives?

The best PayPal Donate Button alternatives depend on whether you want a simple donation button for your website, recurring donations, fundraising campaigns, or a complete donation management system.

If you're looking for something that supporters can click to donate without having to go through PayPal, there are several excellent alternatives.

## Best PayPal Donate Button alternatives

1. Donorbox

Best overall for website donations

Customizable donation buttons and embedded forms.

One-time and recurring donations.

Donor management and automated receipts.

Supports Stripe and PayPal integrations.

Website: donorbox.org

2. Zeffy

Best for zero platform fees

Donation forms and fundraising pages.

Recurring donations.

Donor management and event fundraising.

Funded through optional donor contributions rather than platform fees.

Website: zeffy.com

3. Givebutter

Best all-in-one fundraising solution

Embeddable donation forms and buttons.

Recurring donations and fundraising campaigns.

Peer-to-peer fundraising.

Events, donor management and supporter engagement.

Free platform option with donor tips enabled.

Website: givebutter.com

4. Stripe

Best for simple, direct payments

Payment Links that can act as donation links.

Custom donation amounts.

No need to build a complete checkout system.

Excellent developer tools and integrations.

Particularly interesting if you want to create your own donation button.

Website: Stripe Payment Links

5. Mollie

Best European payment option

Payment links and online checkout.

iDEAL, cards, and other European payment methods.

Easy integration with websites and online stores.

Useful for organizations targeting European donors.

Website: mollie.com

6. Fundraise Up

Best for professional fundraising operations

Optimized donation checkout.

Recurring giving.

Conversion optimization.

Advanced donor analytics and integrations.

Website: fundraiseup.com

## Which one would I choose?

Just want a PayPal-style donate button? Mollie or Stripe.

Want a beautiful donation form embedded in your website? Donorbox.

Want recurring monthly donations? Donorbox.

Want a comprehensive fundraising platform? Givebutter or Zeffy, subject to eligibility.
