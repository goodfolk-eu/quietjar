# Market

Internal only. Never on the website. Estimates built from public anchors and stated assumptions. Replace assumptions with real data the moment we have it. QuietJar figures in euros, sourced figures in their published currency.

## Working numbers

- TAM, all direct fan-to-creator support, global, one-off plus recurring: €3 to 4 billion processed per year
- TAM, one-off only: €1 to 1.5 billion
- SAM, one-off support to site-owning creators and maintainers in markets we can serve: €150 to 300 million per year, Europe roughly half
- SOM, year three, base case: 5,000 active sites, €6 million processed

The SOM swings on two things we control: the published fee and how fast we reach maintainers.

## Sourced anchors

**Creator tipping**

- Creator tipping platforms market: $2.9 billion in 2025, $3.45 billion in 2026, CAGR 18.7%, projected $6.77 billion in 2030 (Research and Markets, Creator Tipping Platforms Market Report 2026)
- Web-based tipping platforms (Patreon, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, Tipeee): $2.39 billion, 38.5% of the market, 2025 (Marketintelo, June 2026)
- Patreon: over $10 billion paid to creators since 2013, milestone passed August 2025 (Patreon via Axios); more than $2 billion per year; $629 million to podcasters in 2025, up 33% (Patreon via Variety, 2026); 8.2 million patrons, 400,000+ creators as of June 2026 (Marketintelo)
- Ko-fi: over 1 million creators (self-reported)
- Buy Me a Coffee: no published volume

Vendor market reports are low-confidence and their definitions differ. Use them for order of magnitude only.

**Open source**

- 60% of maintainers are unpaid; 44% unpaid and would appreciate pay; 25% report income from donation programs; 5% receive direct donations from individuals (Tidelift, 2024 state of the open source maintainer report, 400 respondents)
- 395 million public repositories, up 19% year over year (GitHub Octoverse 2025)

**Processing costs**

- Stripe EU domestic cards 1.5% + €0.25; iDEAL €0.29; SEPA 0.35% capped at €5; international cards +1.5%; currency conversion +1% (checkoutpage.com, payrequest.io, 2026)

## Filters from TAM to SAM

1. Owns a website. Most tipping runs through platforms creators do not own. Assumption: 20% to 30% of tipping volume touches a creator-owned site, almost none of it converting there today.
2. Geography. Europe is roughly a quarter of global creator-economy activity. EU first, UK second, others later.
3. One-off, not membership. Already applied.

## Bottom-up: open source

Assumptions:

- Actively maintained projects with a docs site or landing page: 150,000 to 300,000
- Install rate if painless and good-looking: 10% to 20%
- Average monthly support per small project with a visible button: €20 to 80

200,000 × 15% = 30,000 sites. 30,000 × €50 × 12 = €18 million per year. Range €10 to 40 million. Small, concentrated, reachable, vocal.

## Bottom-up: creators

Assumptions:

- Self-hosted creator and small-publisher sites in EU and UK with an audience that would give: 300,000 to 600,000. Least certain number in this file
- Share using any tip tool today: 10% to 15%
- Share of those adopting an on-page button: 10% to 20%
- Average monthly volume per active site: €50 to 200

450,000 × 12% = 54,000. 54,000 × 15% = 8,000 sites. 8,000 × €100 × 12 = €9.6 million per year at current behaviour. A 50% completion lift puts the same sites near €14 million. The lift is the pitch, so it belongs in the model as a variable.

## SOM scenarios, year three

| | Active sites | Avg monthly volume | Processed per year | Revenue at 5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 1,500 | €60 | €1.1 million | about €55,000 |
| Base | 5,000 | €100 | €6 million | about €300,000 |
| Ambitious | 12,000 | €120 | €17 million | about €850,000 |

Net margin depends on the method mix: iDEAL and wallets versus international cards.

## What moves the number, ranked

1. Install count in the open source segment
2. Proven completion lift
3. The published fee, relative to Buy Me a Coffee's 5%
4. Goals adoption and its effect on time to fund and amount raised (first data point: TCG Pocket Collection Tracker, €97 in 12 hours against €30 a month before)
5. A WordPress plugin, which roughly doubles the reachable creator segment

## Not in this model

- Recurring support. Adding it roughly triples the TAM and puts us against Patreon and Liberapay directly
- The US market
- B2B uses: commercial open source docs, indie SaaS "support the dev" buttons
- Publisher goal campaigns, which can far exceed the per-site averages above

## The only public stat worth using

"Six in ten open source maintainers are unpaid" (Tidelift, 2024). Stronger than any market-size figure.
